Our Journey

Craftsmanship Rooted in Integrity

Founded in Central Florida, JemaOrlando arose from a simple realization: homeowners shouldn't have to hire multiple, uncoordinated contractors to update their interior layout and wire their home safely.

By integrating certified electrical technicians and expert finish carpentry teams, we deliver a cohesive service experience. Whether you want to open up your kitchen space, create a luxury master bath, or upgrade your outdated breaker panel, we ensure your project is completed securely, on budget, and up to the highest local safety standards.

We believe in setting transparent expectations. That's why we offer realistic cost estimates, detailed timeline guides, and continuous project updates from start to finish.
