Safety & Compliance

Certified Electrical Services

Panel Upgrades & Installations

An outdated electrical panel is a safety risk and limits your home's capabilities. Our state-certified electricians replace old fuse boards and obsolete panel boxes (such as Federal Pacific or Zinsco) with safe, modern 150-Amp or 200-Amp breaker systems. We lay down dedicated circuits for heavy appliances, EV vehicle chargers, and home hot tubs.

Ensure your home is prepared to support modern electronics and appliances securely, preventing tripped circuits and fire hazards.

  • 150/200A Panel Replacements
  • EV Charger Installations
  • Dedicated Appliance Lines
  • Surge Protection Systems
  • Sub-Panel Installations
  • Code Compliance Audits

Lighting, Outlets & Troubleshooting

We solve the daily electrical problems that disrupt your peace of mind. Our troubleshooters locate and repair short-circuits, flickering lights, and dead outlets. Brighten up your home with energy-efficient recessed lights or secure your outdoor spaces with smart motion-activated security lights.

Leak Detection & Emergency Pipe Repairs

Hidden pipe leaks can destroy drywall and warp flooring. Our diagnostic plumbers utilize precision leakage detection sensors to pinpoint underground wall leaks without unnecessary tearing. We execute fast copper/PEX pipe leak fixes, clog removals, and whole-house copper-to-PEX repiping.

Don't let a small drip escalate into major structural damage. We solve leaks quickly and safely, restoring water supply lines immediately.

  • Wall & Floor Leak Detection
  • Copper & PEX Pipe Repairs
  • Clogged Drains & Main Cleanouts
  • Whole-House Water Re-piping
  • Pressure Regulating Valves
  • Emergency Shut-Off Installation
